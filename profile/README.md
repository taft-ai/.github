**AI products and engineering that ships.**

TAFT AI is an AI product and engineering company based in India, working with clients across India and globally. It takes on engineering engagements for organizations that want AI done right, and builds its own AI products in stealth.

### What we do

- **Engineering engagements**, embedded with your team or run end-to-end:
  - Production-grade RAG and retrieval
  - Evals and quality infrastructure
  - Agents and tool-use systems
  - Fine-tuning
  - Platform: deployment, monitoring, observability, security
- **Products in stealth**: our own AI products, held to the same production bar as client work.

### Who we build for

- **Indian enterprises**: banks, manufacturing, healthcare and insurance.
- **Public sector**: state governments, central ministries and public utilities.
- **Global teams building from India**, with timezone overlap across EMEA and the US East Coast.

### How we work

- **Senior-led.** The people on the first call stay on the work until it ships.
- **Product-minded.** We design for the end user, and flag a wrong spec before starting.
- **Engineering that ships.** Production code, handed off cleanly with runbooks.
