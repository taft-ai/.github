# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository in the [taft-ai](https://github.com/taft-ai) organization that does not have its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@tafttech.ai** with a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and any proof of concept.

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.

Our machine-readable contact details are at <https://www.tafttech.ai/.well-known/security.txt>.
